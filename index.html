The approach

How every call runs

The same four stages on every booked call, so your pipeline stays predictable and every prospect gets the same care.

  1. 1

    Prepare

    I review the application, the prospect's content history and your offer notes before we ever get on the call.

  2. 2

    Discover

    Open questions about where they are now, where they want to be, and what has kept them from getting there.

  3. 3

    Prescribe

    I connect your program to the exact gap they named, and answer every objection with facts, not pressure.

  4. 4

    Commit

    A clear yes or a clear no, payment taken on the call, and a same-day follow-up for anyone still deciding.

An objection is a question asking to be answered.
